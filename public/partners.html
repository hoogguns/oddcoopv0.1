How the integration works

Wire us into your existing quote flow. No new UI for your sellers.

01

Quote accepted

Your seller accepts a buyback quote on your site. You POST the order to our API.

02

Driver dispatched

We assign a trained {{COOP_NAME}} driver. Your dashboard shows live status updates.

03

Doorstep verify

Driver runs your checklist on-site. IMEI, storage, condition, lock status — all checked against your quote.

04

Pay on match

Specs match? You release payment from your dashboard. Mismatch? Order is flagged for your review before any funds move.
