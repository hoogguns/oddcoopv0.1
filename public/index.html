How {{COOP_NAME}} works

Our couriers use a rigorous 8-point hardware diagnostic on your doorstep. If your device matches the quote, payment is released before they leave your driveway.

01

Get a quote

Your seller gets a buyback quote from your site as usual. {{COOP_NAME}} adds same-day pickup as a delivery option.

02

Driver dispatched

A trained {{COOP_NAME}} driver heads to the seller's address. Average dispatch time under 2 hours.

03

Doorstep verification

The driver runs a full spec check — IMEI, storage, condition, carrier — against your quoted specs.

04

Pack and seal

Device is packed to SOP with tamper-evident materials. Chain of custody is documented.

05

Same-day payment

When specs match: payment fires that day. Mismatch: you review before any funds move.
